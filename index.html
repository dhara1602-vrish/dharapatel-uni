ABOUT ME

Helping businesses look better,
communicate better & grow online.

I'm Dhara Patel, a Digital Marketer and Social Media Manager based in Vadodara, Gujarat.

I work with businesses to build a stronger and more consistent online presence through content, social media and practical digital marketing.

From planning content and creating reels to writing captions, improving local visibility and managing social media pages, I focus on making brands look professional while keeping their communication natural and relatable.

I believe good marketing isn't only about posting more. It's about understanding the business, knowing what the audience needs and creating content that gives people a reason to notice, trust and contact the brand.
